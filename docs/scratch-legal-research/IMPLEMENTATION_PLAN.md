Record Tier 1 outbound handoffs and non-product ledger-clearance requests in [`handoff.md`](handoff.md). Do not edit `docs/source/CHANGELOG.md`, do not bump software versions for legal-research-only gates, and do not clear `verified` rows directly (only Sector 4 Pipeline agents clear verified rows).
